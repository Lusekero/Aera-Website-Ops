# Board Briefing Script (2 Minutes)

Date: 2026-07-24
Audience: Management and institutional leadership

Opening (20 seconds)

Today, I am reporting that we have moved AERA from a traditional website posture to a production-grade Digital Regulatory Service Platform. This is now a strategic service delivery asset that supports institutional digitization, not just online publishing.

What was achieved (50 seconds)

In this release window, we delivered three critical outcomes.

First, reliability: we resolved a production issue that was blocking public access and causing false rate-limit denials, restoring stable citizen-facing service.

Second, security maturity: we implemented a trusted server-to-server request model for SSR traffic using explicit shared-secret validation, and added production fail-fast checks to prevent insecure configuration from going live.

Third, operational governance: we strengthened runbooks, incident recovery paths, and command-based deployment procedures across backend, client, and edge layers, including robust TLS issuance fallback guidance.

Why this matters to AERA (30 seconds)

This directly improves service continuity, public trust, and internal delivery efficiency. We now have a safer and more resilient platform foundation for digitizing regulatory services such as licensing workflows, submission tracking, compliance communication, and future system integrations.

Executive framing (15 seconds)

This should be recognized as the AERA Digital Regulatory Service Portal, an institutional digital service platform with measurable business outcomes, not an IT website task.

Management ask (25 seconds)

I recommend formalizing this as a digital program with:

- Joint ICT and business ownership
- Monthly KPI reviews for uptime, incident recovery, and service performance
- Quarterly roadmap governance for service expansion

With continued executive support, this platform can become a cornerstone for AERA's end-to-end digitization strategy.
